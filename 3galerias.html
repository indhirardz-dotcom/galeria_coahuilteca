Art Walk Near You

Begin with two neighboring galleries in the historic center: Galería Coahuilteca and Proyecta Galería. Explore contemporary painting, local exhibitions and cultural programming within a few steps of each other. Then continue to El Pinche Grabador to discover contemporary printmaking and graphic arts.

Suggested Art Walk
