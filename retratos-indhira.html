From photograph to painting.

The commission is built as a work of art, not as a photographic copy. Indhira begins by selecting the strongest references, studies the structure of the face, develops the drawing and gradually builds the painting through multiple layers.

01

Conversation

You share who the portrait is for, the feeling you want the work to carry and the space where it may live.

02

References

The gallery helps select photographic references with the right light, expression and presence for the painting.

03

Drawing

The portrait begins with structure, proportion and studies before the final painted layers are developed.

04

Painting

Color, depth and skin are built gradually through a slow layered process until the portrait reaches its final presence.

Not simply a likeness. A painting meant to become part of a family’s visual history.
